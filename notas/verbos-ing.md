# Verbos que van con -ing detrás

Si el primer verbo es de esta lista, el segundo entra en **-ing**, nunca en
infinitivo:

    I don't mind workING ✓ · I don't mind work ✗ · I don't mind to work ✗

## Los de uso diario

| Verbo | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| mind | I don't mind working on Sundays. |
| avoid | I avoid riding in the rain. |
| enjoy | She enjoys cooking for friends. |
| keep | He keeps calling me during my shift. |
| finish | I finished reading the report. |
| miss | I miss living near the beach. |
| suggest | She suggested going out for dinner. |
| consider | I'm considering changing jobs. |
| imagine | Imagine living without a phone. |
| practise | I practise speaking every day. |
| risk | You risk losing the order. |
| admit | He admitted lying to her. |
| deny | He denied leaking the documents. |
| recommend | I recommend using a VPN. |
| quit | I quit smoking last year. |
| give up | Don't give up trying. |
| put off | I've been putting off calling the bank. |
| feel like | I don't feel like going out tonight. |
| can't stand | I can't stand waiting in line. |
| can't help | I couldn't help laughing. |
| it's worth | It's worth checking twice. |
| look forward to | I'm looking forward to seeing you. (aquí TO es preposición, no infinitivo) |

## Misma regla, otra cara: después de preposición

Cualquier preposición (without, before, after, by, about, of, for, at…) pide
-ing: *without listening* · *before leaving* · *good at fixing*.
Ver `preposicion-ing.md`.

## Cuidado: los que cambian de significado

Con estos valen las dos formas, pero no significan lo mismo:

| Verbo | + -ing | + to |
|---|---|---|
| stop | I stopped smoking. (dejé de fumar) | I stopped to smoke. (paré para fumar) |
| remember | I remember locking the door. (recuerdo haberla cerrado) | Remember to lock the door. (acuérdate de cerrarla) |
| forget | I'll never forget meeting her. (conocerla) | Don't forget to call her. (llamarla, algo pendiente) |
| try | Try restarting it. (prueba a ver si funciona) | I tried to fix it. (intenté, con esfuerzo) |
